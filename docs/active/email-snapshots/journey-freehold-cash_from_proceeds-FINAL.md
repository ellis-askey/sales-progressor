# Journey: Freehold × Cash from proceeds

Final rewritten email matrix for this journey shape. All phases, every milestone, every variant.

**Voice:** Agency white-labelled "we". Transactional notification style. "Hi [name]" opener, no sign-off, ends at portal link.
**Lexicon:** "related sale" (not "concurrent"). Plain "has to exchange before this purchase can". No em dashes in body copy.
**Shape:** Freehold (no TA7, no management pack, Phase 7 suppressed). Cash from proceeds (mortgage suppressed, deposit transfer suppressed, chain dependency retained). See `SHAPE-NOTES.md`.

## Phase 15 — Exchange

### VM19 — Seller has received confirmation that contracts have exchanged

(Same as freehold-cash_buyer.)

### PM26 — Buyer has received confirmation that contracts have exchanged

**Purchaser — to the buyer:**

**Subject:** Contracts have exchanged, your purchase is now legally binding, 22 Example Road, London SW1A 1AA

```
Hi Alex,

Exchange has happened. Your purchase is now legally binding.

The two solicitors have formally exchanged contracts. Your deposit, funded from your related sale's equity, has been accounted for, and the agreed completion date is locked in. You're now contractually entitled to buy the property on that date, and the seller is contractually obliged to sell.

The next big moment is completion: the balance funds transfer to the seller, ownership formally becomes yours, and the keys are handed over. Your solicitor will be in touch about coordinating the balance funds, most of which come from the proceeds of your related sale completing on the same day. Closer to the date, expect another email with completion-day specifics: timing on the day, where to collect keys, what to do about utilities.

→ View your portal
```
