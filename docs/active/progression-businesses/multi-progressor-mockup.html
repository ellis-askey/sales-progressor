Progression businesses · multi-progressor · proposed UI

One agency, several progressors

Knights Residential is one agency with one team. Who progresses a sale is chosen per sale — Jo, Annalise, in-house, or TSP. Access and the "Managed by" badge already work this way per file; this adds the connect-an-existing-agency handshake and a per-sale progressor choice. Nothing's built yet — this is for your sign-off.

02

Add a client → two routes

Today "Add a client" only sets up a brand-new agency and refuses anyone already on the platform. We split it in two.

Key To Completion · Clients · Add a client

Set up a new agency

They're new to Sales Progressor. We create their agency and email their director an invite to log in. (Today's flow.)

Connect an agency already here NEW

They already use Sales Progressor (their own, or another progressor's). You send a request; their director approves it.

→The "connect" route is the unlock — it's consent-based, so a business can never silently attach itself to an agency it didn't set up.

07

Your calls

1 · Connection model recommended: consent invite

Progressor requests → agency director accepts (as mocked). Keeps it secure and self-serve. Alternative: you/TSP link them from the Command Centre.

2 · Jo & Annalise right now recommended: I link them directly

So they can go live this week, I create the two links directly once the constraint's dropped — no waiting for the full invite UI. The consent flow ships right after for everyone else.

3 · What a connected progressor sees of a shared agency recommended: just their own sales + rate card

Not the agency's internal team roster / branding / settings (those stay with the agency and whoever set them up). Keeps the security invariant intact.

4 · Default progressor on a new sale recommended: explicit pick, no default

When an agency has 2+ progressors, make them choose rather than guess — avoids the silent wrong-business bug that exists today.
