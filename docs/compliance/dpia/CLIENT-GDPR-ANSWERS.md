# Client GDPR Answers — The Sales Progressor

**Companion to:** `DPIA.md` · **Date:** 27 October 2026 · **Owner:** Ellis Askey

Ready-to-use answers to the questions estate agencies and progression businesses ask. Use these in client emails and questionnaires.

---

**Where is our data stored?**
Your data is stored in the EU. Our database and file storage run on Supabase (EU region), and our application runs on Vercel with compute in Dublin, Ireland.

**Who are your sub-processors?**
Our current sub-processors are listed in Schedule B of our Data Processing Agreement: Supabase (hosting/database), Vercel (hosting), SendGrid (email), Anthropic (AI assistance), OpenAI (internal tooling), Stripe (payments), Microsoft and Google (optional mailbox connection and sign-in), Meta (WhatsApp messaging), PostHog (EU analytics), Sentry (error monitoring), Upstash (rate limiting) and Railway. We keep this list current and can provide it on request.

**Do you have a Data Processing Agreement?**
Yes. We have a UK GDPR Article 28 Data Processing Agreement available at `portal.thesalesprogressor.co.uk/legal/dpa`, and we can send it as a PDF. It includes the processing details, our sub-processors, and our security measures.

**What happens to our data when we leave?**
On termination, we return or delete your data in line with the DPA, at your choice. Transaction records are retained for 7 years after completion (a standard conveyancing retention period) and then deleted or anonymised, unless you ask us to delete sooner.

**Is our data encrypted?**
Yes. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Sensitive stored credentials receive an additional layer of application-level encryption.

**Which AI provider do you use?**
Anthropic's Claude, accessed through their secure commercial API (not the public consumer tool). Requests are made from our servers; the provider has no access to your account.

**Is client information used to train AI?**
No. Under Anthropic's commercial API terms, your content is not used to train any AI models.

**How long does the AI provider retain information?**
We do not store the content submitted to the AI explainer or its response; we keep only a record that the feature was used. The provider processes the content transiently to generate the explanation and does not use it for training.

**Can AI functionality be disabled?**
Yes. It can be switched off for your account from your settings.

**Have you completed a DPIA?**
Yes. We have completed a Data Protection Impact Assessment covering the platform and its AI features, and we can share a summary on request.

**How are data breaches reported?**
In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify you without undue delay and, in any event, within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, with the information you need to meet your own reporting obligations. This is set out in our DPA.
