# Logging In and Your First Day

## Logging in

Go to `portal.thesalesprogressor.co.uk` and sign in with your email and password. If your agency uses Microsoft 365, you can also sign in with your Microsoft account using the "Sign in with Microsoft" button.

If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page. A reset link will be emailed to you.

## What you'll see first — the Hub

After logging in you land on the **Hub**, your daily dashboard. If you have just joined and no files have been added yet, the Hub shows a prompt to add your first sale. The charts and pipeline stats fill in once you have active files.

See [The Hub](../01-running-your-pipeline/hub.md) for a full explanation of everything on the Hub page.

## Your first actions

### 1. Update your profile

Go to **Settings** (the cog icon in the sidebar). On the profile card, check your name and email are correct and add your phone number.

### 2. Verify a sending address (directors)

If you want chase emails to appear as coming from your work email address (e.g. `jane@youragency.co.uk`) rather than a generic address, go to **Settings → Sending addresses** and verify your work email. This involves clicking a confirmation link sent to that address.

### 3. Add your first sale

Click **New sale** in the top right of the Hub. Fill in the property address, the buyer and seller contact details, and whether this is a freehold or leasehold property (and whether the buyer is purchasing with a mortgage or cash). These answers determine which milestones apply automatically.

### 4. Send portal links to your clients

Once a file is created, go to the **Overview tab** of the property file. You'll find a portal link section for both the vendor and purchaser. Send the link to each client so they can track progress.

## Navigation

The sidebar on the left gives access to:

- **Hub** — daily dashboard
- **Files** (or All Files) — your full transaction list
- **Work Queue** — all reminders across your pipeline
- **Completions** — files near or past exchange
- **Analytics** — pipeline trends
- **Settings** — profile, team, sending addresses
