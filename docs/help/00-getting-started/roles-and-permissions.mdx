# Roles and permissions

Sales Progressor uses two roles for agency users: **director** and **negotiator**. Both use the same interface, but directors have additional management capabilities.

The role determines which files you see and which actions you can take. A flag called **Can view all files** can extend a negotiator's visibility — covered below.

| Capability | Director | Negotiator |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| See all agency files | ✓ | Only with **Can view all files** |
| Add team members | ✓ | — |
| Remove team members | ✓ | — |
| Manage agency settings | ✓ | — |
| Create property files | ✓ | ✓ |
| Edit own files | ✓ | ✓ |
| Confirm milestones | ✓ | ✓ |
| Send portal links | ✓ | ✓ |
| Generate chase emails | ✓ | ✓ |
| Invite a director | — | Only when no director yet |

## Director

Directors are the account owners for an agency. An agency can have more than one director, and all directors have equal authority — there is no senior or primary director.

A director can also be assigned to property files as the responsible negotiator and run their own pipeline alongside their oversight role.

Directors can:

- View and edit every file in the agency
- Add negotiators to the team
- Remove negotiators from the team
- Toggle whether individual negotiators can view all agency files
- Manage verified sending email addresses
- Change agency-wide settings

### Limitations

- Remove other directors
- Remove themselves from the agency

To remove a director from an agency, contact Sales Progressor support. Self-service director removal is not currently available.

## Negotiator

Negotiators handle day-to-day progression work. By default, a negotiator only sees files they are assigned to — files they created or that a director has assigned to them.

A negotiator can also create their own agency directly when signing up. In that case, they have temporary account ownership until they invite a director. Once the director joins, the standard director/negotiator structure applies.

Negotiators can:

- Create new property files
- View and edit files they are assigned to
- Mark milestones complete or not required
- Generate and send chase emails
- Add notes and manual tasks
- Send portal links to clients
- Update their own profile
- Invite their director to join the agency (only if the agency does not yet have a director)

### Limitations

- View files assigned to other negotiators (unless granted full visibility — see below)
- Add or remove other team members
- Change agency-wide settings
- Promote themselves to director

## Granting full file visibility

A director can give an individual negotiator visibility of every file in the agency. This is useful for senior negotiators who act as deputies. The setting is **Can view all files**, found on each team member in **Settings → Team**.

To enable full file visibility for a negotiator:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Find the negotiator in the team list
3. Toggle **Can view all files** on

The change takes effect on the negotiator's next page load. They do not need to sign out and back in.

Granting full file visibility does not give a negotiator any team management or settings access. It only changes which files they can see and edit.

## Inviting a director

If your agency was created by a negotiator and does not yet have a director, the negotiator can invite one from **Settings → Invite your director**.

To invite a director:

1. Go to **Settings → Invite your director**
2. Enter the director's name and email address
3. Click **Send invitation**

The director receives an email with a link to join. They can sign in with Google, Microsoft, or set a password. Once they accept, the **Invite your director** section disappears from Settings — only one director invitation is needed per agency.

## Adding a negotiator

Directors add negotiators from **Settings → Team**.

To add a negotiator:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Click **Add a negotiator**
3. Enter their name, email address, and a temporary password
4. Click **Create account**

Share the temporary password with the negotiator directly — by text, in person, or however you normally communicate. The system does not send it for them. They can change it from **Settings → My profile** once they have signed in.

## Checking your role

Your role is shown on **Settings → My profile**, alongside your name and email address.

A simpler check: if you have access to **Settings → Team**, you are a director. Negotiators do not see this section.

## Constraints

- An agency must have at least one director or one negotiator
- Negotiators cannot promote themselves to director
- Roles cannot be changed for users with pending invitations — they must accept the invitation first
- Directors cannot remove other directors or themselves; contact Sales Progressor support to remove a director
