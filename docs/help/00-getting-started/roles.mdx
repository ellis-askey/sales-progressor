# Roles and permissions

Sales Progressor uses two roles for agency staff: director and negotiator. Both use the same interface, but directors have additional management capabilities.

---

## Director

Directors are the account owners for an agency branch. An agency can have more than one director, and all directors have equal authority.

Directors can:
- View and edit every file in the agency
- Add and remove negotiators
- Control whether individual negotiators can view all files or only their own
- Manage verified sending addresses
- Delete the agency account

Directors cannot:
- See files belonging to other branches — each branch is scoped separately
- Reduce the agency to zero directors (one must remain at all times)

---

## Negotiator

Negotiators handle day-to-day progression work.

Negotiators can:
- Create new property files
- Mark milestones complete and not required
- Generate and send chase emails
- Add notes and manual tasks
- Send portal links to clients
- Update their own profile

Negotiators cannot:
- Access Settings → Team or manage other users
- Verify sending addresses
- Delete the agency account
- Promote themselves to director

---

## Granting full file visibility

By default, a negotiator can only see files they created themselves. To allow a negotiator to see every file in the branch:

1. Go to **Settings → Team**.
2. Open the negotiator's profile.
3. Toggle **Can view all files**.

The negotiator needs to sign out and back in for the change to take effect.

---

## Changing roles

A director can promote a negotiator to director, or demote another director to negotiator, from **Settings → Team**.

One constraint: an agency must always have at least one director. You cannot demote the last remaining director.

Roles cannot be changed for users with a pending invitation — they must accept the invitation first.

---

## Checking your role

Your role is shown on **Settings → Profile**, alongside your name and email.

If you have access to **Settings → Team**, you are a director. Negotiators do not see this section.

---

## Limitations

- An agency must have at least one director at all times.
- Negotiators cannot promote themselves.
- Roles cannot be changed for users with pending invitations — they must accept first.
