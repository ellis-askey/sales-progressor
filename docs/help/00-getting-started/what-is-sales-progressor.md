# What is Sales Progressor?

Sales Progressor is a sales progression platform for UK estate agents. It manages residential property transactions from offer-accepted through to exchange and completion — replacing manual chasing, scattered spreadsheets, and inconsistent communication with a single structured system.

## What it replaces

Most agencies manage sales progression through a combination of:

- Paper or spreadsheet chase logs
- Email folders and phone call notes
- Mental notes about which solicitor is holding things up
- Reminder sticky notes or calendar entries

Sales Progressor replaces all of this with a structured pipeline where every milestone, every chase, every contact interaction, and every status change is recorded in one place.

## The two sides of a sale

Every residential sale involves two legal processes running in parallel:

**Vendor side** — the seller's solicitor prepares the contract pack, handles ID checks, answers enquiries from the buyer's solicitor, and ultimately signs and returns contracts to exchange.

**Purchaser side** — the buyer's solicitor orders searches, raises enquiries, receives mortgage offer (if applicable), reviews the survey, and signs contracts to exchange.

Both sides must reach readiness before exchange can happen. Sales Progressor tracks 20 vendor milestones (VM1–VM20) and 27 purchaser milestones (PM1–PM27) for every file.

## How the product is used

There are two service tiers:

**Self-managed** — your agency uses the platform to manage your own files. You and your negotiators log in, mark milestones, generate chase emails, and use the client portal to keep buyers and sellers updated.

**Outsourced** — Sales Progressor's internal team progresses the file on your behalf. You add the sale, and the progression work is handled for you.

## What you'll find in the platform

- **Hub** — your daily dashboard: pipeline health, attention items, exchange forecast
- **Property files** — individual transaction pages with milestones, reminders, to-do items, and a full activity log
- **Client portal** — a private link you send to each buyer or seller so they can track progress
- **Work Queue** — all reminders across your pipeline in one view
- **Analytics** — pipeline trends over time
- **Team management** — add negotiators, control who sees which files
