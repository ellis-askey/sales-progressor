# What is Sales Progressor?

Sales Progressor is a sales-progression system for UK residential estate agencies. It tracks every property transaction from the moment an offer is accepted through to legal completion, with structured milestones for both the vendor's side and the purchaser's side.

It's built for directors and negotiators who progress sales after they've been agreed.

## What you will find

- **Hub** — daily dashboard: pipeline health, attention items, exchange forecast
- **Property files** — individual transaction pages with milestones, reminders, to-do items, and an activity log
- **Client portal** — a private link for each buyer or seller to track their own progress
- **Reminders** — all active chases across your pipeline in one view
- **Completions** — files that have exchanged and are heading to completion
- **Analytics** — pipeline trends, fee tracking, and conversion rates

## The two sides of a sale

Every residential sale has two legal threads running in parallel.

**Vendor side** — the seller's solicitor prepares the contract pack, handles ID checks, answers enquiries, and signs contracts to exchange.

**Purchaser side** — the buyer's solicitor orders searches, raises enquiries, receives the mortgage offer (if applicable), and signs contracts to exchange.

The estate agency sits between the two sides — chasing solicitors for updates, keeping buyers and sellers informed, and pushing to keep both sides on track.

Both sides must reach readiness before exchange can happen. Sales Progressor tracks the vendor side and the purchaser side as parallel sequences of milestones — about 20 on the vendor side, 27 on the purchaser side.

## Two service tiers

**Self-managed** — your agency uses Sales Progressor directly. Your negotiators sign in, mark milestones, generate chase emails, and send portal links to buyers and sellers.

**Outsourced** — Sales Progressor's own team progresses files on your behalf. You add the sale; the progression work is handled for you.
