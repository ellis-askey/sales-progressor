# Your first day

## Signing in

Go to the login page and sign in with your email and password. If your agency uses Microsoft 365, use **Continue with Microsoft**. For a forgotten password, use **Forgot password?** — a reset link will be emailed to you.

If you arrived via an invitation email from a director (or from someone you invited), the link in that email will take you through signup before you reach the hub.

## What you'll see first

After signing in you land on the Hub — your daily dashboard. If no files have been added yet, the Hub will be mostly empty and prompts you to add your first sale.

The charts and pipeline stats populate as you add active files.

See [The Hub](../01-running-your-pipeline/hub) for a full description of what appears there.

## First actions

### 1. Check your profile

Go to **Settings** (the cog icon in the sidebar). Confirm your name and email are correct and add your phone number if it is not already there.

### 2. Verify a sending address (directors only)

To have chase emails sent from your own work address (e.g. `olivia@hartwellestates.co.uk`), verify it in **Settings → Sending addresses**. You'll receive a confirmation email at that address.

See [Verified sending addresses](../07-notifications/verified-sending-addresses) for the full steps.

### 3. Add your first sale

Click **New sale** in the top right of the Hub. Enter the property address, buyer and seller contact details, whether the property is freehold or leasehold, and whether the buyer is purchasing with cash or a mortgage. These answers set which milestones apply to the file automatically.

<PropertyFileHeaderExample />

### 4. Send portal links to your clients

Once a file is created, go to the **Overview tab**. The Contacts card shows a portal link for each buyer and seller. Send the link early — once contacts have it, they can check progress without contacting you.
