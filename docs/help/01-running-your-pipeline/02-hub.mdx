# The hub

The Hub is your working dashboard in Sales Progressor. It loads each time you sign in and is organised around what needs attention today — not a comprehensive list of files, but the activity, alerts, and forecasts that are most relevant right now.

The page answers two questions: what needs action today, and how is the pipeline performing? Every section answers one of those.

## What changes by role

Directors and negotiators see the same Hub layout. What changes is the scope of the data.

Directors — and negotiators who have been granted **Can view all files** — see agency-wide figures: all active files, all attention items across the team, the combined pipeline value. Negotiators without that permission see only their own assigned files.

See [Roles and permissions](../00-getting-started/02-roles-and-permissions.mdx) for how visibility is configured and what each role can do.

## Today's diary

The diary appears at the top of the Hub on any day where something is scheduled. It shows exchanges and completions due today, drawn from the expected exchange dates and completion dates set on your active files.

Each row shows the property address and a type badge — green for a completion, coral for an exchange. Completions appear first. Each row links directly to the file.

The diary does not appear on days with nothing scheduled. It is not shown in the empty state.

## Needs your attention

<HelpSplit>
<HelpSplitText>
The attention section shows reminders that are due or overdue on your active files. Three urgency levels:

- **Escalated** — a chase task on this file has been flagged as escalated
- **Overdue** — the reminder's due date has passed
- **Due today** — the reminder is due today

Items appear with escalated at the top, then overdue, then due today. Up to three rows show on the Hub. The full list of all active reminders lives in the Work Queue — click **Reminders** in the section header to go there.

When nothing is due, the section shows a green dot and "No reminders due right now. All clear."

## Pipeline health

The pipeline health card shows four figures from your active files, a summary of what's coming up shortly, and a flag for stalled files.

### Active files

The total number of files with an active status — offers accepted and in progress, not yet exchanged. A "+ N this month" badge appears when new files have been added since the start of the calendar month. Clicking the stat takes you to All Files.

### Exchanging soon

Files where the expected exchange date falls within the next 30 days. Clicking the stat takes you to the Completions page.

### Need attention

The count of distinct files that have at least one overdue or escalated reminder. This counts files, not individual reminders — a file with three overdue reminders counts as one here. The stat colours red when any escalated items exist, amber when there are overdue items, and default text otherwise. Clicking the stat takes you to the Work Queue.

### Pipeline value

The sum of purchase prices on your active files, formatted as £k, £m, or £bn. Files without a price entered contribute zero — so the figure reflects the total for files where prices have been set.

### Coming up

Three quick-glance figures sit below the four stats:

- **N exchanging this week** — active files with an exchange date in the next seven days, not yet exchanged
- **N completing this week** — active files with a completion date in the next seven days, not yet completed
- **£X closing this month** — the combined purchase price of files expected to exchange this calendar month

Each figure links to the All Files page filtered to the matching subset.

### Stalled files

A file is considered stalled when no milestone has been confirmed on it in the last 14 days and it has not yet exchanged. The count appears below the coming up strip. Clicking through opens the Work Queue.

When all files have recent activity, the row shows "All files have recent activity" in muted text.

## Momentum

<HelpSplit>
<HelpSplitText>
The momentum ring compares this calendar month's exchanges to last month's. The ring fills proportionally to the percentage — full means you are at least on pace with last month. The percentage is printed at the centre.

- **Ahead of last month** — ring fills fully, shown in green
- **Below last month** — ring fills partially, shown in amber

The momentum figure uses calendar-month boundaries, not rolling 30 days. On the first of a new month, the count resets to zero regardless of recent exchange activity.

When there is no previous month to compare against — during your first month of activity — the ring shows a placeholder: "No comparison yet."
</HelpSplitText>

## Exchange forecast

<HelpSplit>
<HelpSplitText>
The exchange forecast shows the next five weeks of expected exchanges, starting from the current week. Each bar represents active files with an exchange date in that week. Files that have already exchanged are excluded.

The current week's bar renders at full opacity. Future bars fade progressively — dates further ahead are more likely to shift.

When no exchange dates are set across all five weeks, the card prompts you to add expected dates to your active files.

Below the chart: the count for this week and for the next 30 days. When the current week has at least one exchange due, a prompt appears to make sure those files are ready.

## Service split

<HelpSplit>
<HelpSplitText>
The service split donut shows how your active files are divided between self-managed and outsourced.

- **Self-managed** (coral) — your team is progressing these files directly
- **With progressor** (amber) — the Sales Progressor team is handling these on your behalf

When outsourced files are present, the card shows an estimate of the agent hours the outsourced service is saving your team that week. This is an approximate figure based on a fixed assumption per file, not a measurement.

## Recent activity

The activity ribbon at the bottom of the Hub shows the single most recent event across your files — either the latest communication (email sent, call logged, update received) or the latest milestone confirmed, whichever happened more recently. Clicking the ribbon opens the file.

The ribbon only appears when there is activity to show. It does not appear in the empty state.

## Refreshing the Hub

The "As of HH:MM" timestamp in the page header shows when the Hub last loaded. The Hub does not update automatically while you have it open — click the refresh icon next to the timestamp to reload everything. All seven data fetches run again and the page reflects the current state of your pipeline.

## What to do next

- Add expected exchange dates to your active files so the forecast and coming up strip show useful figures
- Open the Work Queue to see the full list of reminders — the Hub shows only the top three
