# Analytics

The Analytics page (`/agent/analytics`) shows pipeline performance over time. Use it to understand trends across your business rather than the day-to-day state of individual files.

## What you'll find

- **Exchange and completion counts** — how many sales exchanged or completed in a given period
- **Average time to exchange** — how long files typically take from offer-accepted to exchange
- **Pipeline value over time** — how your total active pipeline has grown or contracted
- **Milestone completion rates** — which milestones consistently take the longest to complete across your files

## Time periods

Use the period selector to switch between views: last 30 days, last 90 days, last 12 months, or a custom date range.
