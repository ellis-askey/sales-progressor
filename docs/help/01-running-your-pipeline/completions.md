# Completions View

The Completions view (`/agent/completions`) shows files that are at or near exchange — where both the vendor and purchaser exchange-gate milestones (VM18 and PM25) are complete, or where an exchange date has been set.

Use this view to monitor your most advanced files and make sure nothing slips at the final hurdle.

## What appears here

A file appears in Completions when:
- Both VM18 (seller's solicitor ready to exchange) and PM25 (buyer's solicitor ready to exchange) are marked complete, or
- The expected exchange date is within the next 14 days

## Actions from this view

From each file in the Completions view you can:
- Click through to the full property file
- Mark VM19 (contracts exchanged — seller) and PM26 (contracts exchanged — buyer) once exchange happens
- Then mark VM20 (sale completed) and PM27 (purchase completed) once completion occurs
