# The Hub — Your Daily Dashboard

The Hub is the first page you see after logging in. It shows the current state of your pipeline at a glance: where your files stand, which need attention, and what's coming up.

## Pipeline health

The **Pipeline health** card shows four numbers across the top:

| Stat | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active files** | Sales currently in progression — not yet exchanged or withdrawn |
| **Exchanging soon** | Files where both parties are marked ready to exchange (VM18 and PM25 both complete) |
| **Need attention** | Files with at least one reminder that is overdue, escalated, or due today |
| **Pipeline value** | Sum of the agreed sale prices across all active files |

Below these four numbers, a **Coming up** strip shows:
- How many files are exchanging this week
- How many are completing this week  
- The total value closing this month

If any files have had no activity for 14 or more days, a stalled files warning appears. Clicking it takes you to the [Work Queue](work-queue.md).

## Needs your attention

This section lists files where something requires action — a chase is overdue, escalated, or due today. Up to three items are shown; click "Reminders" to see the full list in the Work Queue.

Each item is colour-coded:

- **Escalated** (red) — the chase has been sent multiple times without a response and has been escalated
- **Overdue** (amber) — the grace period and repeat interval have both passed without the milestone being completed
- **Due today** (coral) — a chase is due today

Clicking an item takes you directly to the Reminders tab of that file.

## Today's diary

If any files have their exchange or completion date set to today, they appear in a **Today's diary** strip at the top of the Hub. Exchange dates are shown in coral, completion dates in green.

## Momentum

The **Momentum** card shows how many exchanges you've achieved this month compared to last month. The ring fills proportionally — a full ring means you're on pace or ahead.

## Exchange forecast

The **Exchange forecast** chart shows how many files are expected to exchange over the next four to five weeks, based on the expected exchange dates set in each file. Use this to spot busy weeks and plan your workload.

## Service split

The **Service split** donut shows what proportion of your active files are self-managed versus being progressed by Sales Progressor's team. If you have files with the progressor service, the card estimates the agent hours saved.

## Recent activity ribbon

At the bottom of the Hub, a ribbon shows the most recent piece of activity across your pipeline — a milestone marked, a note added, a chase sent — with a link to that file.

## Refreshing the data

The Hub shows a timestamp ("As of 14:22") and a refresh button. Click the refresh button to reload the data without a full page refresh.
