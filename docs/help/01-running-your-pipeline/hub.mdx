# The Hub

The Hub is the first page you see after logging in. It shows the current state of your pipeline at a glance: where files stand, what needs attention, and what is coming up.

## Pipeline health

The pipeline health card shows four numbers:

| Stat | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active files** | Sales currently in progression — not yet exchanged or withdrawn |
| **Exchanging soon** | Files where both exchange-gate milestones (VM18 and PM25) are complete |
| **Need attention** | Files with at least one reminder that is overdue, escalated, or due today |
| **Pipeline value** | Sum of agreed sale prices across all active files |

Below those numbers, a Coming up strip shows how many files are exchanging this week, how many are completing this week, and the total value closing this month.

If any files have had no activity for 14 or more days, a stalled files warning appears. Clicking it takes you to the Reminders page.

## Needs your attention

This section lists files where action is required — a chase is overdue, escalated, or due today. Up to three items appear; click "Reminders" to see the full list.

Each item is colour-coded:
- **Escalated** (red) — the chase has been sent multiple times and hit the escalation threshold
- **Overdue** (amber) — the grace period and repeat interval have passed without the milestone being completed
- **Due today** (coral) — a chase is due today

Clicking an item opens the Reminders tab of that file.

## Today's diary

Files with an exchange or completion date set to today appear in a Today's diary strip at the top of the Hub. Exchange dates are shown in coral; completion dates in green.

## Momentum

The Momentum card shows how many exchanges you have achieved this month compared to last month. The ring fills proportionally.

## Exchange forecast

The Exchange forecast chart shows how many files are expected to exchange over the next four to five weeks, based on the expected exchange dates set in each file.

## Service split

The Service split donut shows the proportion of active files that are self-managed versus being progressed by Sales Progressor's team. If outsourced files are present, the card estimates the agent hours saved.

## Recent activity ribbon

A ribbon at the bottom of the Hub shows the most recent piece of activity across your pipeline — a milestone marked, a note added, a chase sent — with a link to that file.

## Refreshing the data

The Hub shows a timestamp and a refresh button. Click it to reload the data without a full page refresh.
