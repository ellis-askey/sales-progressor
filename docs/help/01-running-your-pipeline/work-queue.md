# Work Queue — Reminders in One Place

The Work Queue (`/agent/work-queue`) collects every active reminder across all your files into a single prioritised list. Use it as your morning action list — work through what needs chasing before moving on to new files.

## Urgency levels

Reminders in the Work Queue are sorted by urgency:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Escalated** | The chase has repeated multiple times and hit the escalation threshold. Needs direct intervention — a phone call, not just another email. |
| **Overdue** | The grace period has passed and the milestone is still incomplete, but not yet escalated. Send a chase. |
| **Due today** | A chase is scheduled for today based on the reminder calendar. |

## What each row shows

Each reminder row shows:
- The property address
- The name of the reminder (e.g. "Seller ID & AML completed")
- The urgency badge
- A link to the Reminders tab of that file

## Working through the queue

Click any row to go directly to the Reminders tab of that file. From there you can:
- Mark the milestone complete (which clears the reminder)
- Generate and send a chase email
- Add a note about the call or conversation

After taking action, return to the Work Queue and continue down the list.

## Stalled files

Files with no activity for 14 or more days also appear in the Work Queue (highlighted separately). A stalled file isn't necessarily behind — it may be waiting on something legitimate — but it's worth reviewing to confirm nothing has been missed.
