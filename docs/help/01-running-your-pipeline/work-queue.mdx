# Reminders

The Reminders page collects every active chase across all your files into a single prioritised list. The stat row at the top shows a count breakdown — overdue, due today, and coming up — so you can see the shape of the queue before working through it.

## How items are classified

| Classification | Condition |
|---|---|
| **Overdue** | The chase date has passed, or the reminder has been escalated to red |
| **Due today** | The chase is scheduled for today |
| **Coming up** | The chase is due within the next three business days |

Items still within their grace period do not appear in the list.

## Stalled files

The Reminders page also surfaces stalled files — files with no activity for 14 or more days — in a separate strip at the top. A stalled file is not necessarily behind; it may be legitimately waiting. It is worth reviewing to confirm nothing has been missed.

## Working through the queue

Click any reminder to go to the Reminders tab of that file. From there you can:
- Generate and send a chase email
- Mark the milestone complete (which resolves the reminder)
- Add a note about a call or conversation

Return to the Reminders page and continue down the list.
