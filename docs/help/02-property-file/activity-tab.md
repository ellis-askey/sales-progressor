# Activity Tab

The Activity tab is the full history of everything that has happened on a file — in chronological order, most recent first. Every meaningful action is recorded here automatically.

## What appears in the activity log

- **Milestones marked complete** — who marked it and when
- **Milestones unmarked** — if a completion was reversed
- **Notes added** — internal notes by you or a colleague
- **Chase emails sent** — the recipient, the milestone, and the send timestamp
- **Portal link sent** — when a client was sent their portal link
- **Manual tasks created and completed**
- **Status changes** — e.g. file moved to Exchanged or Withdrawn
- **Contact details updated**

## Adding an internal note

Click **Add note** to write a free-text note. Notes are only visible to your agency — they're not shown to clients in the portal. Use notes to record phone conversations, explain why a milestone was marked in a particular way, or flag something for a colleague.

## Email parse

If you receive a relevant email outside the platform (e.g. a solicitor replies by email), you can paste the email text into the email parse widget in the Activity tab. The system reads the email and suggests which milestones or reminders it might relate to, helping you quickly update the file.

## Filtering the timeline

Use the filter buttons at the top of the timeline to show only certain types of entries: milestones, notes, emails, or status changes.
