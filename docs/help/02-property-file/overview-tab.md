# Overview Tab

The Overview tab is the first thing you see when you open a property file. It gives you a summary of the file without requiring you to scroll through every milestone.

## What's on the Overview tab

### Property header

At the top: the property address, sale price, current status badge, and overall progress percentage. The progress bar reflects weighted milestone completion across both the vendor and purchaser sides.

### Contacts card

Shows the buyer and seller contacts — names, email addresses, and phone numbers if added. Each contact has a **portal link** button next to their name. Click it to copy or send the portal link for that contact.

The contact card also shows when the client last visited their portal, if they have opened it.

### Solicitors card

Shows the vendor solicitor and purchaser solicitor details. Click to expand and edit if details change during the transaction.

### Quick milestone summary

A condensed view of how many milestones are complete, in progress, and not yet started on each side (vendor and purchaser). Click "View all milestones" to jump to the [Milestones tab](milestones-tab.md).

### Active reminders widget

Shows the top active reminders — chaseable items that are due, overdue, or escalated. Clicking a reminder takes you to the full [Reminders tab](reminders-tab.md).

### Recent activity widget

The last few entries from the activity log — notes added, milestones marked, emails sent. Clicking "View all" takes you to the [Activity tab](activity-tab.md).

## Editing property details

Click the edit icon next to the property address or sale price to update them. You can also update the expected exchange date from the Overview tab.
