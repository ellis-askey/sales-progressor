# Reminders Tab

The Reminders tab shows every active reminder for this file — chases that are due, overdue, or escalated, along with those still within their grace period. It also shows the history of chases already sent.

## What is a reminder?

Each milestone has an associated reminder rule. When a milestone becomes Available, its reminder clock starts. After the grace period passes, the reminder becomes active — it appears in the Reminders page and signals that a chase is due.

See [How the reminder system works](../04-reminders-and-chasing/how-reminders-work) for the full explanation.

## Reminder states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Within grace period** | The milestone just became available — no chase needed yet |
| **Due today** | Today is the chase date |
| **Overdue** | The chase date has passed and the milestone is still incomplete |
| **Escalated** | The chase has repeated multiple times and crossed the escalation threshold |
| **Snoozed** | Deferred to a later date |
| **Resolved** | The milestone was marked complete |

## Actions on a reminder

**Generate a chase email** — click Chase to open the chase drawer. Choose a channel (email or WhatsApp) and a tone, generate a draft, review it, and send.

**Mark the milestone complete** — if you have confirmation from a call, mark the milestone complete directly from this tab. This resolves the reminder immediately.

**Snooze** — if the party is waiting on something with a known date, snooze the reminder. The chase is suppressed until the snooze date.

**Add a note** — record what was said or agreed. Notes appear in the Activity tab and are visible to your team only.

## Chase history

Below the active reminders, the Reminders tab shows all chases sent for this file: date, recipient, milestone, and channel.
