# Purchaser Milestones — PM1 to PM27

Purchaser milestones track the buyer's solicitor process from instruction through to completion. There are 27 in total.

For cash buyers, PM5, PM6, and PM11 are marked Not Required automatically. For freehold properties, PM12 is also Not Required automatically.

---

## PM1 — Buyer instructed solicitor

The buyer has chosen their solicitor and formally instructed them to act on the purchase.

**Predecessor:** None — Available immediately when the file is created.  
**Grace period:** 2 days  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM2 — Buyer memorandum of sale received

The buyer's solicitor has received the memorandum of sale from the agent.

**Predecessor:** None — Available immediately.  
**Grace period:** 1 day  
**Repeat chase:** every 2 days  
**Escalation:** after 2 chases

---

## PM3 — Buyer ID and AML completed

The buyer has completed identity verification and anti-money-laundering checks with their solicitor.

**Predecessor:** PM1  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM1  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM4 — Buyer money paid on account

The buyer has paid monies on account to their solicitor (typically to cover search fees and other upfront disbursements).

**Predecessor:** PM1  
**Grace period:** 5 days after PM1  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM5 — Buyer mortgage application submitted *(mortgage buyers only)*

The buyer has submitted their mortgage application to a lender.

**Predecessor:** None — Available immediately.  
**Grace period:** 3 days  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

**Not Required** automatically for cash buyers.

---

## PM6 — Lender valuation booked *(mortgage buyers only)*

The lender has booked a valuation survey of the property.

**Predecessor:** PM5  
**Grace period:** 7 days after PM5  
**Repeat chase:** every 7 days  
**Escalation:** after 2 chases

**Not Required** automatically for cash buyers.

---

## PM7 — Draft contract pack received by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has received the draft contract pack from the seller's solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM7 (draft contract pack issued by seller's solicitor)  
**Grace period:** 2 days after VM7  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

This is a cross-side predecessor — the buyer can't receive the pack until the seller's solicitor has issued it.

---

## PM8 — Searches ordered

The buyer's solicitor has ordered the local authority, drainage, and environmental searches.

**Predecessor:** PM7  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM7  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM9 — Survey booked

The buyer has booked a structural survey or homebuyer's report on the property.

**Predecessor:** None — Available immediately.  
**Grace period:** 7 days  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

**Can be marked Not Required manually** if the buyer has decided not to commission a survey. Marking PM9 Not Required automatically sets PM10 (survey report received) to Not Required too.

---

## PM10 — Survey report received

The buyer has received the survey report.

**Predecessor:** PM9  
**Grace period:** 7 days after the survey booking date  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

The reminder for PM10 uses the actual survey date (entered when marking PM9 complete) rather than a relative offset from file creation.

---

## PM11 — Mortgage offer received *(mortgage buyers only)*

The buyer has received a formal mortgage offer from their lender.

**Predecessor:** PM6 (lender valuation booked)  
**Grace period:** 14 days after PM6  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

**Not Required** automatically for cash buyers. The reminder uses the valuation date.

---

## PM12 — Management pack received (buyer) *(leasehold only)*

The buyer's solicitor has received the management pack from the seller's side (via VM9).

**Predecessor:** VM9 (management pack received — seller)  
**Grace period:** 3 days after VM9  
**Repeat chase:** every 10 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

**Not Required** automatically for freehold properties.

---

## PM13 — Search results received

The buyer's solicitor has received the results of all searches ordered in PM8.

**Predecessor:** PM8  
**Grace period:** 21 days after PM8 (searches typically take this long)  
**Repeat chase:** every 7 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM14 — Initial enquiries raised by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has raised their initial enquiries on the contract pack and property information.

**Predecessor:** PM7 (draft contract pack received)  
**Grace period:** 5 days after PM7  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

Once PM14 is complete, VM10 (initial enquiries received by seller's solicitor) becomes Available.

---

## PM15 — Initial replies received by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has received the initial replies from the seller's solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM12 (initial replies issued by seller's solicitor)  
**Grace period:** 14 days after VM12  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM16 — Initial replies reviewed by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has reviewed and acknowledged the initial replies.

**Predecessor:** PM15  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM15  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM17 — Further enquiries raised by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has raised additional (further) enquiries, typically after reviewing the initial replies and search results.

**Predecessor:** PM16  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM16  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

Once PM17 is complete, VM13 (further enquiries received by seller's solicitor) becomes Available.

---

## PM18 — Further replies received by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has received the further replies from the seller's solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM15 (further replies issued by seller's solicitor)  
**Grace period:** 14 days after VM15  
**Repeat chase:** every 7 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM19 — Further replies reviewed by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has reviewed the further replies and confirmed they are satisfied.

**Predecessor:** PM18  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM18  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM20 — All enquiries satisfied

The buyer's solicitor has confirmed that all enquiries are resolved and the legal due diligence is complete.

**Predecessor:** PM19  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM19  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM21 — Final report received by buyer

The buyer's solicitor has sent the buyer a final report on title — the legal summary of the property and the terms of the purchase.

**Predecessor:** PM20  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM20  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM22 — Contract documents received by buyer

The buyer has received the contract documents from their solicitor for signing.

**Predecessor:** PM21  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM21  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM23 — Signed contracts returned by buyer

The buyer has signed and returned the contract documents to their solicitor.

**Predecessor:** PM22  
**Grace period:** 5 days after PM22  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM24 — Deposit transferred by buyer

The buyer has transferred the exchange deposit (typically 10% of the purchase price) to their solicitor.

**Predecessor:** PM23  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM23  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## PM25 — Buyer's solicitor ready to exchange ⭐ Exchange gate

The buyer's solicitor has confirmed they are in a position to exchange contracts — all enquiries are satisfied, the buyer has signed contracts, the deposit is held, and the mortgage offer is in place.

**Predecessor:** PM24  
**Grace period:** 2 days after PM24  
**Repeat chase:** every 2 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases  
**Requires exchange readiness:** Yes — both PM25 and VM18 must be Complete before exchange can be confirmed.

This is the purchaser-side exchange gate. Exchange cannot happen until both PM25 and VM18 are complete. See [Exchange gates](exchange-gates.md).

---

## PM26 — Contracts exchanged (buyer)

Contracts have been formally exchanged. The completion date is now legally binding.

**Predecessor:** PM25  
**Grace period:** 1 day after PM25  
**Repeat chase:** every 2 days  
**Escalation:** after 1 chase

---

## PM27 — Purchase completed

The purchase has completed — the buyer's solicitor has transferred funds and the title has been registered. The buyer now owns the property.

**Predecessor:** PM26  
**Grace period:** 1 day after PM26  
**Repeat chase:** every 1 day  
**Escalation:** after 2 chases

Marking PM27 complete should trigger a status change to Completed on the file.
