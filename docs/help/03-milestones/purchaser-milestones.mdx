# Purchaser Milestones

Purchaser milestones track the buyer's solicitor process from instruction through to completion. There are 27 in total.

For cash buyers, PM5, PM6, and PM11 are marked Not Required automatically. For freehold properties, PM12 is also Not Required automatically.

---

## PM1 — Buyer instructed solicitor

The buyer has chosen their solicitor and formally instructed them to act on the purchase.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="None — available immediately" grace="2 days" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM2 — Buyer memorandum of sale received

The buyer's solicitor has received the memorandum of sale from the agent.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="None — available immediately" grace="1 day" repeat="every 2 days" escalation="after 2 chases" />

---

## PM3 — Buyer ID and AML completed

The buyer has completed identity verification and anti-money-laundering checks with their solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM1" grace="3 days after PM1" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM4 — Buyer money paid on account

The buyer has paid monies on account to their solicitor, typically to cover search fees and other upfront disbursements.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM1" grace="5 days after PM1" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM5 — Buyer mortgage application submitted *(mortgage buyers only)*

The buyer has submitted their mortgage application to a lender.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="None — available immediately" grace="3 days" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

Not Required automatically for cash buyers.

---

## PM6 — Lender valuation booked *(mortgage buyers only)*

The lender has booked a valuation survey of the property.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM5" grace="7 days after PM5" repeat="every 7 days" escalation="after 2 chases" />

Not Required automatically for cash buyers.

---

## PM7 — Draft contract pack received by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has received the draft contract pack from the seller's solicitor. This is a cross-side predecessor — the buyer cannot receive the pack until VM7 (draft contract pack issued) is complete.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM7" grace="2 days after VM7" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM8 — Searches ordered

The buyer's solicitor has ordered the local authority, drainage, and environmental searches.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM7" grace="3 days after PM7" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM9 — Survey booked

The buyer has booked a structural survey or homebuyer's report on the property.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="None — available immediately" grace="7 days" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

**Can be marked Not Required manually** if the buyer has decided not to commission a survey. Marking PM9 Not Required automatically sets PM10 to Not Required too.

---

## PM10 — Survey report received

The buyer has received the survey report.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM9" grace="7 days after the survey booking date" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

The reminder uses the actual survey date entered when marking PM9 complete, not a relative offset from file creation.

---

## PM11 — Mortgage offer received *(mortgage buyers only)*

The buyer has received a formal mortgage offer from their lender.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM6" grace="14 days after PM6" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

Not Required automatically for cash buyers. The reminder uses the valuation date.

---

## PM12 — Management pack received (buyer) *(leasehold only)*

The buyer's solicitor has received the management pack from the seller's side (via VM9).

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM9" grace="3 days after VM9" repeat="every 10 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

Not Required automatically for freehold properties.

---

## PM13 — Search results received

The buyer's solicitor has received the results of all searches ordered in PM8.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM8" grace="21 days after PM8" repeat="every 7 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

Searches typically take three weeks — the 21-day grace period reflects this.

---

## PM14 — Initial enquiries raised by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has raised their initial enquiries on the contract pack and property information. Once PM14 is complete, VM10 (initial enquiries received by seller's solicitor) becomes available.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM7" grace="5 days after PM7" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM15 — Initial replies received by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has received the initial replies from the seller's solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM12" grace="14 days after VM12" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM16 — Initial replies reviewed by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has reviewed and acknowledged the initial replies.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM15" grace="3 days after PM15" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM17 — Further enquiries raised by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has raised additional enquiries, typically after reviewing the initial replies and search results. Once PM17 is complete, VM13 (further enquiries received by seller's solicitor) becomes available.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM16" grace="3 days after PM16" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM18 — Further replies received by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has received the further replies from the seller's solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM15" grace="14 days after VM15" repeat="every 7 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM19 — Further replies reviewed by buyer's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has reviewed the further replies and confirmed they are satisfied.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM18" grace="3 days after PM18" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM20 — All enquiries satisfied

The buyer's solicitor has confirmed that all enquiries are resolved and legal due diligence is complete.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM19" grace="3 days after PM19" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM21 — Final report received by buyer

The buyer's solicitor has sent the buyer a final report on title — the legal summary of the property and the terms of the purchase.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM20" grace="3 days after PM20" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM22 — Contract documents received by buyer

The buyer has received the contract documents from their solicitor for signing.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM21" grace="3 days after PM21" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM23 — Signed contracts returned by buyer

The buyer has signed and returned the contract documents to their solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM22" grace="5 days after PM22" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM24 — Deposit transferred by buyer

The buyer has transferred the exchange deposit (typically 10% of the purchase price) to their solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM23" grace="3 days after PM23" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## PM25 — Buyer's solicitor ready to exchange ⭐ Exchange gate

The buyer's solicitor has confirmed they are in a position to exchange contracts — all enquiries satisfied, buyer has signed contracts, deposit held, mortgage offer in place.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM24" grace="2 days after PM24" repeat="every 2 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

Both PM25 and VM18 must be Complete before exchange can happen. See [Exchange gates](exchange-gates).

---

## PM26 — Contracts exchanged (buyer)

Contracts have been formally exchanged. The completion date is now legally binding.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM25" grace="1 day after PM25" repeat="every 2 days" escalation="after 1 chase" />

---

## PM27 — Purchase completed

The purchase has completed — the buyer's solicitor has transferred funds and the title has been registered. The buyer now owns the property.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM26" grace="1 day after PM26" repeat="every 1 day" escalation="after 2 chases" />

Marking PM27 complete triggers the file status to change to Completed.
