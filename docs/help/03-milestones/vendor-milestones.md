# Vendor Milestones — VM1 to VM20

Vendor milestones track the seller's solicitor process from instruction through to completion. There are 20 in total.

For leasehold properties, VM8 and VM9 are active. For freehold properties, they are marked Not Required automatically.

---

## VM1 — Seller instructed solicitor

The seller has chosen their solicitor and formally instructed them to act on the sale.

**Predecessor:** None — Available immediately when the file is created.  
**Grace period before first chase:** 2 days  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

Mark this complete as soon as you have confirmation the seller's solicitor has been instructed. This is the starting gun for most of the vendor side — VM3 and VM4 both unlock when VM1 is complete.

---

## VM2 — Seller memorandum of sale received

The seller's solicitor has received the memorandum of sale (MOS) from the agent.

**Predecessor:** None — Available immediately.  
**Grace period:** 1 day  
**Repeat chase:** every 2 days  
**Escalation:** after 2 chases

---

## VM3 — Seller welcome pack received

The seller's solicitor has sent the client a welcome pack including ID requirements and the property information forms.

**Predecessor:** VM1  
**Grace period:** 3 days after VM1  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM4 — Seller ID and AML completed

The seller has completed identity verification and anti-money-laundering checks with their solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM1  
**Grace period:** 3 days after VM1  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM5 — Seller property information forms received

The seller has received the TA6 and TA10 forms (property information forms and fittings and contents form) from their solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM3  
**Grace period:** 5 days after VM3  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM6 — Seller property information forms returned

The seller has completed and returned the property information forms to their solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM5  
**Grace period:** 5 days after VM5  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM7 — Draft contract pack issued

The seller's solicitor has prepared and issued the draft contract pack to the buyer's solicitor. This is a major milestone — it unlocks the bulk of the purchaser side.

**Predecessor:** VM6  
**Grace period:** 5 days after VM6  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

Once VM7 is complete, PM7 (draft contract pack received by buyer's solicitor) becomes Available on the purchaser side.

---

## VM8 — Management pack requested *(leasehold only)*

The seller's solicitor has requested the management pack from the freeholder or managing agent.

**Predecessor:** None — Available immediately (leasehold files only).  
**Grace period:** 3 days  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

**Not Required** automatically for freehold properties.

---

## VM9 — Management pack received *(leasehold only)*

The management pack has been received by the seller's solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM8  
**Grace period:** 21 days after VM8 (managing agents typically take this long)  
**Repeat chase:** every 7 days  
**Escalation:** after 2 chases

**Not Required** automatically for freehold properties. Once VM9 is complete, PM12 (management pack received — buyer) becomes Available.

---

## VM10 — Initial enquiries received by seller's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has raised their initial enquiries and the seller's solicitor has received them.

**Predecessor:** PM14 (initial enquiries raised by buyer's solicitor)  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM14  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM11 — Seller initial replies provided to solicitor

The seller has provided their responses to the initial enquiries to their solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM10  
**Grace period:** 5 days after VM10  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM12 — Initial replies issued by seller's solicitor

The seller's solicitor has formally issued the initial replies to the buyer's solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM11  
**Grace period:** 2 days after VM11  
**Repeat chase:** every 3 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

Once VM12 is complete, PM15 (initial replies received by buyer's solicitor) becomes Available.

---

## VM13 — Further enquiries received by seller's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has raised further enquiries (raised after reviewing the initial replies).

**Predecessor:** PM17 (further enquiries raised by buyer's solicitor)  
**Grace period:** 3 days after PM17  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

Not all sales have further enquiries. If no further enquiries are raised, mark VM13 Not Required (consult your manager about the correct procedure for your agency).

---

## VM14 — Seller further replies provided to solicitor

The seller has provided their responses to the further enquiries.

**Predecessor:** VM13  
**Grace period:** 5 days after VM13  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM15 — Further replies issued by seller's solicitor

The seller's solicitor has issued the further replies to the buyer's solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM14  
**Grace period:** 2 days after VM14  
**Repeat chase:** every 2 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

Once VM15 is complete, PM18 (further replies received by buyer's solicitor) becomes Available.

---

## VM16 — Contract documents issued to seller

The seller's solicitor has sent the final contract documents to the seller for signing.

**Predecessor:** VM7 (draft contract pack issued)  
**Grace period:** 3 days after VM7  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM17 — Signed contracts returned by seller

The seller has signed the contract documents and returned them to their solicitor.

**Predecessor:** VM16  
**Grace period:** 5 days after VM16  
**Repeat chase:** every 5 days  
**Escalation:** after 3 chases

---

## VM18 — Seller's solicitor ready to exchange ⭐ Exchange gate

The seller's solicitor has confirmed they are in a position to exchange contracts — all enquiries are satisfied, the seller has signed contracts, and funds are in order.

**Predecessor:** VM17  
**Grace period:** 2 days after VM17  
**Repeat chase:** every 2 days  
**Escalation:** after 2 chases  
**Requires exchange readiness:** Yes — both VM18 and PM25 must be Complete before exchange can be confirmed.

This is the vendor-side exchange gate. Exchange cannot happen until both VM18 and PM25 are complete. See [Exchange gates](exchange-gates.md).

---

## VM19 — Contracts exchanged (seller)

Contracts have been formally exchanged. The completion date is now legally binding.

**Predecessor:** VM18  
**Grace period:** 1 day after VM18  
**Repeat chase:** every 2 days  
**Escalation:** after 1 chase

---

## VM20 — Sale completed (seller)

The sale has completed — the seller's solicitor has transferred the title and the proceeds of sale. Keys have been handed over.

**Predecessor:** VM19  
**Grace period:** 1 day after VM19  
**Repeat chase:** every 1 day  
**Escalation:** after 2 chases

Marking VM20 complete should trigger a status change to Completed on the file.
