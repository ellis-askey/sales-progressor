# Vendor Milestones

Vendor milestones track the seller's solicitor process from instruction through to completion. There are 20 in total.

For leasehold properties, VM8 and VM9 are active. For freehold properties, they are marked Not Required automatically.

---

## VM1 — Seller instructed solicitor

The seller has chosen their solicitor and formally instructed them to act on the sale.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="None — available immediately" grace="2 days" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

VM3 and VM4 both unlock when VM1 is marked complete.

---

## VM2 — Seller memorandum of sale received

The seller's solicitor has received the memorandum of sale from the agent.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="None — available immediately" grace="1 day" repeat="every 2 days" escalation="after 2 chases" />

---

## VM3 — Seller welcome pack received

The seller's solicitor has sent the client a welcome pack including ID requirements and property information forms.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM1" grace="3 days after VM1" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM4 — Seller ID and AML completed

The seller has completed identity verification and anti-money-laundering checks with their solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM1" grace="3 days after VM1" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM5 — Seller property information forms received

The seller has received the TA6 and TA10 forms (property information and fittings and contents) from their solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM3" grace="5 days after VM3" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM6 — Seller property information forms returned

The seller has completed and returned the property information forms to their solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM5" grace="5 days after VM5" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM7 — Draft contract pack issued

The seller's solicitor has prepared and issued the draft contract pack to the buyer's solicitor. Once VM7 is complete, PM7 (draft contract pack received by buyer's solicitor) becomes available.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM6" grace="5 days after VM6" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM8 — Management pack requested *(leasehold only)*

The seller's solicitor has requested the management pack from the freeholder or managing agent.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="None — available immediately (leasehold files only)" grace="3 days" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

Not Required automatically for freehold properties.

---

## VM9 — Management pack received *(leasehold only)*

The management pack has been received by the seller's solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM8" grace="21 days after VM8" repeat="every 7 days" escalation="after 2 chases" />

Not Required automatically for freehold properties. Once VM9 is complete, PM12 (management pack received — buyer) becomes available.

---

## VM10 — Initial enquiries received by seller's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has raised their initial enquiries and the seller's solicitor has received them.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM14" grace="3 days after PM14" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM11 — Seller initial replies provided to solicitor

The seller has provided their responses to the initial enquiries to their solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM10" grace="5 days after VM10" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM12 — Initial replies issued by seller's solicitor

The seller's solicitor has formally issued the initial replies to the buyer's solicitor. Once VM12 is complete, PM15 (initial replies received by buyer's solicitor) becomes available.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM11" grace="2 days after VM11" repeat="every 3 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM13 — Further enquiries received by seller's solicitor

The buyer's solicitor has raised further enquiries after reviewing the initial replies.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="PM17" grace="3 days after PM17" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

Not all sales have further enquiries. If none are raised, VM13 can be marked Not Required.

---

## VM14 — Seller further replies provided to solicitor

The seller has provided their responses to the further enquiries.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM13" grace="5 days after VM13" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM15 — Further replies issued by seller's solicitor

The seller's solicitor has issued the further replies to the buyer's solicitor. Once VM15 is complete, PM18 (further replies received by buyer's solicitor) becomes available.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM14" grace="2 days after VM14" repeat="every 2 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM16 — Contract documents issued to seller

The seller's solicitor has sent the final contract documents to the seller for signing.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM7" grace="3 days after VM7" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM17 — Signed contracts returned by seller

The seller has signed the contract documents and returned them to their solicitor.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM16" grace="5 days after VM16" repeat="every 5 days" escalation="after 3 chases" />

---

## VM18 — Seller's solicitor ready to exchange ⭐ Exchange gate

The seller's solicitor has confirmed they are in a position to exchange contracts — all enquiries satisfied, seller has signed contracts, funds in order.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM17" grace="2 days after VM17" repeat="every 2 days" escalation="after 2 chases" />

Both VM18 and PM25 must be Complete before exchange can happen. See [Exchange gates](exchange-gates).

---

## VM19 — Contracts exchanged (seller)

Contracts have been formally exchanged. The completion date is now legally binding.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM18" grace="1 day after VM18" repeat="every 2 days" escalation="after 1 chase" />

---

## VM20 — Sale completed (seller)

The sale has completed — the seller's solicitor has transferred the title and the proceeds of sale. Keys have been handed over.

<MilestoneMetaBlockExample predecessor="VM19" grace="1 day after VM19" repeat="every 1 day" escalation="after 2 chases" />

Marking VM20 complete triggers the file status to change to Completed.
