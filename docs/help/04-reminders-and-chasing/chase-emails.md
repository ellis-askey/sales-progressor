# Generating Chase Emails

Sales Progressor can draft and send chase emails on your behalf. The email is generated using AI — it knows which milestone is being chased, the property address, and the relevant parties — so the draft is contextually accurate and ready to review.

## How to generate a chase email

1. Go to the **Reminders tab** of the property file, or navigate there from the **Work Queue**.
2. Click **Chase** next to an active reminder.
3. A draft email appears. Review the content — the AI typically gets it right, but you can edit any detail.
4. Confirm the recipient (the solicitor or client associated with the milestone).
5. Click **Send**.

## What address the email comes from

If you have a verified sending address set up in Settings, the email is sent from your work email address (e.g. `jane@youragency.co.uk`). The client or solicitor will see it as coming from you directly.

If no verified address is set up, the email is sent from a generic Sales Progressor address on your behalf.

See [Verified sending addresses](../07-notifications/verified-sending-addresses.md) for how to set this up.

## Chase limits

To prevent accidental email flooding, there is a limit on how many chase emails can be sent per hour. If you hit this limit, the system will tell you when you can try again.

## Sending rate

The chase email system limits bulk sending to prevent spam flags. If you have many reminders due at once, work through them in batches across the day rather than trying to send all at once.

## Recording a manual chase

If you chased by phone rather than email, you can record it manually. From the Reminders tab, click **Log a call** (or similar) and enter what was said. This resets the reminder repeat clock just as a sent email would, and the chase is logged in the Activity tab.
