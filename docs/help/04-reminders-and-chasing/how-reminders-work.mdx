# How the Reminder System Works

Every milestone has an associated reminder rule. When a milestone becomes Available, that rule starts tracking it — checking whether it is completed within the expected timeframe and escalating if it is not.

## The lifecycle of a reminder

### 1. Milestone becomes Available

The moment a milestone becomes Available — either at file creation (for milestones with no predecessor) or when a predecessor is marked complete — its reminder clock starts.

### 2. Grace period

Every rule has a grace period measured in days. During this window, the reminder is silent. No chase appears in the Reminders page or Reminders tab.

For example: VM3 (seller's welcome pack received) has a 3-day grace period after VM1. Mark VM1 complete on a Monday and the reminder for VM3 does not appear until Thursday.

### 3. First chase

When the grace period expires, the reminder becomes active. It appears in:
- The **Reminders** page (prioritised by urgency)
- The **Reminders tab** of the file
- The **Hub** "Needs your attention" section (top three items)

### 4. Repeat chases

If the milestone is still not complete after the first chase, the reminder repeats according to the rule's repeat interval. VM5, for example, has a 3-day repeat interval — chase on Monday and the next chase appears Thursday.

### 5. Escalation

After a set number of chases without the milestone being completed, the reminder escalates. It turns red in the Reminders page and on the Hub. The escalation threshold is set per milestone — most escalate after 3 chases; exchange-readiness milestones (VM18, PM25) after 2.

An escalated reminder signals that routine email chasing has not worked. A phone call, or escalation to a more senior person at the relevant firm, is usually needed.

### 6. Resolution

When the milestone is marked Complete, the reminder resolves automatically and disappears from the Reminders page and Reminders tab. The chase history — how many chases were sent and when — is preserved in the Activity tab.

## Anchor-based timing

Most reminders start their grace period from when the predecessor milestone was completed. A few use event dates: PM10 (survey report received) uses the actual survey booking date entered when PM9 is marked complete, making the timing more accurate for events with a known date.
