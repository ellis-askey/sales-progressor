# What Your Client Sees

When a client opens their portal link, they land on a clean, mobile-friendly page that presents their transaction progress in plain English. Here's exactly what they see.

## The progress view

At the top: a progress indicator — a visual representation of how far through the process they are. Below that, a heading describing the current stage (e.g. "Getting things moving" or "Enquiries and due diligence").

The stage descriptions change as milestones are completed:

| Stage | When it applies |
|---|---|
| **Getting started** | Early in the transaction — solicitors just being instructed |
| **Getting things moving** | Solicitors engaged, welcome packs sent, ID in progress |
| **Pack issued and due diligence underway** | Contract pack issued, searches ordered, enquiries beginning |
| **Deep in enquiries** | Enquiries in active exchange between solicitors |
| **Ready to exchange** | Both sides approaching or at the exchange gates |
| **Exchanged** | Contracts formally exchanged, completion date set |
| **Completed** | Transaction fully completed |

Below the stage heading, the portal shows a short description of what's happening at this stage and a list of the main things that need to happen next.

## The updates timeline

The Updates page (accessible from the portal navigation) shows a timeline of recent activity — when milestones were completed, any messages from the agent, and key dates. This gives the client the "what's been happening" view between conversations.

## The exchange page

Once the transaction is approaching exchange, the Exchange page becomes accessible. It shows:
- What is still outstanding on the vendor side before exchange
- What is still outstanding on the purchaser side before exchange
- The expected exchange date (if set)

This helps clients understand why exchange hasn't happened yet — and crucially, which side any delay is on.

## What language is used

The portal uses plain, non-technical English. Milestone codes (VM7, PM25) are never shown. Legal jargon is translated:

- "Draft contract pack issued" → "Your solicitor has prepared the contract pack and sent it to the buyer's solicitor"
- "Initial enquiries raised" → "The buyer's solicitor has raised questions about the property and contract"
- "Seller's solicitor ready to exchange" → "Your solicitor has confirmed they're ready to exchange contracts"

## What clients cannot do

The current portal is read-only for clients. They can view progress and updates, but cannot:
- Send messages directly to you through the portal
- Mark anything on their own
- See your internal notes
- See the other side's progress details in full
