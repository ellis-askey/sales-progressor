# What the Portal Is

The client portal is a private, secure web page you send to each buyer or seller. It gives them a real-time view of where their transaction stands — without them needing to call you every day asking "what's happening?"

## Why the portal matters

Buyers and sellers don't understand the legal process, and they don't need to. What they do need is reassurance that their sale or purchase is moving forward. The portal translates the milestone data into plain English summaries and progress indicators that non-lawyers can follow.

Keeping clients updated through the portal:
- Reduces the volume of inbound calls asking for status updates
- Builds trust in your agency's professionalism
- Gives clients something tangible to check between your conversations

## What clients see on the portal

The portal has four main views:

### Progress page

The headline view — a progress indicator showing how far along the transaction is, a plain-language description of the current stage, and a list of the next steps that need to happen.

The stage is determined automatically from which milestones are complete. For example, a vendor who has completed VM1–VM3 but not yet VM7 is at the "solicitor engaged" stage. The portal describes this in accessible language, not milestone codes.

### Updates page

A timeline of what has happened recently — milestones completed, key events, messages from you. This gives clients the "what's been happening while I've been waiting" view.

### Exchange page

Once the transaction is close to exchange (both exchange gates are approaching completion), the Exchange page becomes the focus. It shows what is still outstanding on both sides before exchange can happen.

### Completion page

Shown once the file is marked Completed. A confirmation view summarising the completed transaction.

## What clients cannot see

- Internal notes (these are for your team only)
- Milestone codes or technical legal terminology
- Other clients' portals or any data outside their own transaction
- Anything from a file they weren't associated with

## Security

Each client gets their own unique portal link. The link is tied to that contact on that transaction — it cannot be used to access any other file. There is no password; the link itself is the authentication.

Portal links do not expire, but if a transaction falls through or is withdrawn, the portal will reflect the updated status.
