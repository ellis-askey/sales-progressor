# What the Portal Is

The client portal is a private, secure web page sent to each buyer or seller. It gives them a real-time view of where their transaction stands — in plain English, without requiring them to call you.

## Why the portal matters

Buyers and sellers do not understand the legal process, and they do not need to. What they need is reassurance that their sale or purchase is moving forward. The portal translates milestone data into plain-language summaries and progress indicators that non-lawyers can follow.

Keeping clients updated through the portal:
- Reduces inbound calls asking for status updates
- Builds trust in your agency's professionalism
- Gives clients something to check between conversations

## What clients see on the portal

The portal has four main views.

**Progress** — the headline view. A progress indicator showing how far along the transaction is, a plain-language description of the current stage, and a list of the next steps that need to happen.

**Updates** — a timeline of recent activity: milestones completed, key events. This gives clients the "what's been happening" view between conversations.

**Exchange** — once the transaction is approaching exchange, this view shows what is still outstanding on both sides before exchange can happen, and the expected exchange date if set.

**Completion** — shown once the file is marked Completed. A confirmation view summarising the completed transaction.

## What clients cannot see

- Internal notes (for your team only)
- Milestone codes or technical legal terminology
- Other clients' portals or any data outside their own transaction

## Security

Each client gets their own unique portal link. The link is tied to that contact on that transaction only. There is no password — the link itself is the authentication.

Portal links do not expire, but if a transaction falls through or is withdrawn, the portal reflects the updated status.
