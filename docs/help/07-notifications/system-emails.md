# What Emails the System Sends

Sales Progressor sends emails automatically in certain circumstances. This article describes which emails are sent, to whom, and when.

## Emails sent to your clients

### Portal link email

**When:** When you click "Send by email" from the portal link section on the Overview tab.  
**To:** The client (buyer or seller) at the email address on their contact record.  
**Content:** A brief introduction explaining what the portal is and a button to access it.  
**From:** Your verified sending address if set up; otherwise a generic Sales Progressor address.

### Progress update emails *(if configured)*

Clients can receive periodic update emails when significant milestones are completed. This is configured per file.

## Emails sent to you (agents)

### Password reset

**When:** You click "Forgot password?" on the login page.  
**To:** The email address associated with your account.  
**Content:** A time-limited reset link.

### Sending address verification

**When:** You add a new sending address in Settings.  
**To:** The email address you're trying to verify.  
**Content:** A confirmation link. Clicking it verifies the address and allows the platform to send emails on your behalf from that address.

## What emails are NOT sent automatically

The system does **not** send:
- Chase emails automatically — you generate and send these manually from the Reminders tab
- Milestone completion notifications to clients — the portal is how clients track progress; no email is sent every time you mark a milestone
- Alerts to solicitors — all solicitor communication is through your manually generated chase emails
