# What Emails the System Sends

Sales Progressor sends emails automatically in several situations. This article describes each type — who receives it, when, and what it contains.

---

## Milestone notification emails

Every time you mark a milestone complete, the system sends a personalised email to the relevant parties on that transaction.

**Who receives it:**

| Recipient | When they receive it |
|---|---|
| Seller (vendor contact) | On any vendor milestone or buyer milestone that affects their side |
| Buyer (purchaser contact) | On any purchaser milestone or seller milestone that affects their understanding |
| Your agency (negotiator) | On most milestones — brief agent-facing version |

The email content is written from each recipient's point of view. The seller gets seller-framed copy ("your solicitor has done X"). The buyer gets buyer-framed copy ("the seller has done X, here's what it means for your purchase"). They are not generic — each recipient gets a version written for them.

**What the email contains:**

- Property address in the header
- A headline describing what happened
- One or two sentences explaining what it means
- What happens next (where relevant)
- A button linking back to their portal

**Special cases:**

- **Exchange (VM19 / PM26)** — both sides receive a richer "Exchange Completion Pack" explaining what happens between exchange and completion and what to expect on moving day.
- **Completion (VM20 / PM27)** — congratulations email with the confirmed completion date.
- **Ready to exchange (VM18 / PM25)** — "Exchange is imminent" email confirming solicitors are in position.

Contacts must have an email address and a portal link to receive these emails. Contacts without a portal token are excluded.

---

## Portal link email

**When:** You click "Send by email" from the portal link section on the Overview tab.
**To:** The buyer or seller at the email address on their contact record.
**Content:** A brief introduction to the portal with a button to access it.
**From:** Your verified sending address if one is set up; otherwise a generic Sales Progressor address.

---

## Emails sent to you (agents)

### Password reset

**When:** You click "Forgot password?" on the login page.
**To:** The email address associated with your account.
**Content:** A time-limited reset link.

### Sending address verification

**When:** You add a new sending address in Settings.
**To:** The email address you are trying to verify.
**Content:** A confirmation link. Clicking it verifies the address.

---

## What emails are not sent automatically

- **Chase emails** — these are generated and sent manually from the Reminders tab. The system never chases solicitors on your behalf without you reviewing and sending the email.
- **Bulk updates** — there is no daily digest or weekly summary sent to buyers or sellers.
- **Internal notes** — activity feed entries are logged internally only; they are not emailed to anyone.
