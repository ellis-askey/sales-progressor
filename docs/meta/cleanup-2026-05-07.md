## What's now live in production

### Negotiator invitation flow (token-based)

Directors can now invite negotiators by email rather than creating accounts with a temporary password. The flow:

1. Director opens Settings → Team and types the negotiator's name and email
2. The negotiator receives a branded invitation email with a time-limited link
3. The negotiator clicks the link, sees a landing page confirming who invited them and which agency
4. They choose to sign in with Google, Microsoft, or set a password
5. Their account is created and linked to the agency automatically
6. The director sees the negotiator's status flip from "Pending" to "Active" in the team list

Pending invitations are visible in the team list. Directors can resend or cancel pending invitations. The invitation expires after 7 days.

Files: `app/invite-negotiator/`, `app/actions/invite-negotiator.ts`, `app/api/agent/team/pending/`, `components/agent/TeamListView.tsx`, `components/agent/TeamManagement.tsx`, `lib/email/negotiator-invitation.ts`, `lib/auth/validate-negotiator-invitation-token.ts`

### Director invitation flow (B1–B3)

Negotiators can invite their director to join the platform. Three-phase flow:

- **B1**: Backend infrastructure — `DirectorInvitation` model, token generation, invitation email
- **B2**: Negotiator-side UI — invitation form inside Settings → Team
- **B3**: Director-side acceptance — branded landing page, Google/Microsoft/password options

`/invite/*` routes are now public and accessible to unauthenticated visitors.

### Google and Microsoft (Azure AD) sign-in

Existing users can now sign in with Google or Microsoft in addition to email/password. OAuth is configured with `allowDangerousEmailAccountLinking: true` — signing in with an OAuth provider that matches an existing account email links the accounts rather than creating a duplicate.

New users who sign in via OAuth for the first time are routed to a signup-completion screen to confirm their details before accessing the app.

Files: `lib/auth.ts`, `components/login/GoogleIcon.tsx`, `components/login/MicrosoftIcon.tsx`
